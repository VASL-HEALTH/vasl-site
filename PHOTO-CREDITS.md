One deviation worth recording: `rural-porch-man.jpg` is saved at **quality 79**
rather than 82. It is a 1400x2096 portrait, more than twice the pixel area of
the landscapes, and q82 produced 431,542 bytes -- over the 400 KB bar. q79 is
the smallest step that clears 400,000 bytes, so it satisfies the bar on either
reading of "400 KB". Every other file is q82.
