Vasl Keep

Eligible members keep the coverage they qualify for. Every reply sorted for a navigator.

Keep supports Medicaid plans under the community engagement rule of 80 hours a month. Members answer by text. Each reply is sorted into a category. Navigators see who needs help, and the plan sees the totals.

Built. Two live end-to-end runs, real texts delivered.

How Keep works

One text in. One clear next step out.

01 · Member

Replies by text

No app, no portal login.

02 · Sorting

The reply is sorted

Into one of seven reply categories, or “no reply”.

03 · Navigator

Works the queue

The members who need a person, first.

04 · Plan

Sees the totals
