The member’s path

The member’s path.

A young person is enrolled the day they are discharged from the emergency department, referred by a school, or invited by a community partner. There is no app to download: the platform is text-first.

Vasl’s text, every day

built from their replies
03
VLAP reads the reply
When a reply matters, the partner’s clinician sees it, with the exact words.
04
Peer community and coach
Peer communities organized around shared identity and experience, moderated where needed by licensed staff. A coach, who is a licensed social worker employed by the partner, assigned the same day.
Grow
Grow, a structured eight-week curriculum they can work through with their coach.
05
Warm handoff
A warm handoff to the partner’s own licensed clinician when that is what they need. Crisis runs on the partner’s own protocol. Vasl delivers no therapy.

Privacy by role

Each person sees only what their role needs.

Members never see scores. Only the partner’s credentialed clinicians see clinical detail. Each partner sees its own population’s outcomes, the numbers it reports to Medicaid.
