Claims have clearinghouses. Clinician notes have the electronic health record. What patients write has had nothing.

The texts, check-ins and messages where people say how they are really doing are read by tools built on textbook English. Those tools are least accurate for Black, Brown, LGBTQ+, rural and Medicaid patients. We built VLAP on them first.

Language-based depression models were over three times less predictive for Black users (Penn, PNAS 2024).

What a clinician sees

A named signal, the exact words behind it, and the needs in her own notes.

VLAP surfaces, flags and informs. The clinician decides. Non-device clinical decision support under the 21st Century Cures Act, Section 3060.

Running on VLAP today

Two workflows, each built around a measure health plans already track.

Vasl Bridge

A daily text from the emergency department to the first follow-up visit. No app. VLAP reads every reply, and a coach reaches out when someone goes quiet.

10 of 10 steps passed live, with real texts delivered.

Vasl Keep

Eligible Medicaid members keep the coverage they qualify for. Members answer by text, each reply is sorted, and navigators reach the people who need a person first.
