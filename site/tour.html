When a tile says not captured yet

That is the system refusing to guess. Where nothing has been measured, no number is shown: never a zero, never a plausible average. If you go looking for the fabricated value, there isn’t one.

Measured on real clinical notes

VLAP reads social needs in clinical notes. Trained on notes from two hospitals and scored with the official n2c2 2022 scorer, it finds alcohol, tobacco, drug use, employment and housing needs, and whether each is current, at F1 0.79 to 0.91 on the second hospital’s test notes. Your sites are where we measure it on your own population next.
