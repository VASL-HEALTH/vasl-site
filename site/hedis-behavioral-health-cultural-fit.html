Health plans and systems

Seven days after a mental health emergency visit. Most patients go quiet.

The HEDIS follow-up measure Medicaid plans are scored on, where patients drop off, and what reading their replies changes.

Only 31.2% of young people on Medicaid make it to a follow-up visit within seven days of a mental health emergency visit (Hoffmann et al., Pediatrics 2023). The other two thirds leave the emergency department and go quiet in the week that matters most.

The measure

Follow-Up After Emergency Department Visit for Mental Illness, known as FUM, is the HEDIS measure for that week. It counts members 6 and older who have a follow-up visit within 7 days, and within 30 days, of an emergency department visit for mental illness or intentional self-harm. Medicaid plans are scored on it.

FUM does not measure whether a patient got better. It measures whether they came back. That makes it an engagement measure, and engagement is decided in the days between discharge and the first visit.

Where patients drop off

Most follow-up programs send reminders and count who answered. A reply like “been staying in my room. ain’t nobody checking for me anyway” counts as an answer. It is also the reply a clinician most needs to see.

Everyone else sends reminders. We understand the replies.

How Vasl Bridge works on it

  • The patient is enrolled by text before leaving the emergency department. No app.
  • A check-in goes out every day. Outbound texts carry no health information.
  • VLAP reads every reply, and a clinician sees the flag and the exact sentence behind it.
  • If the patient goes quiet, a coach reaches out. Silence is a signal too.
  • The first visit is recorded against the seven-day measure.

Bridge has passed 10 of 10 steps live, with real texts delivered. For health systems it is priced at $150 per patient enrolled plus $150 per completed seven-day visit, and one avoided boarding stay ($2,264) pays for eight enrollments and seven completed visits. For Medicaid plans it is $0.50 per covered member per month.
