Security

Patient text stays inside Vasl. Bring your security team.

VLAP is a language model Vasl built and owns, running on Vasl infrastructure in AWS. Patient text is read by VLAP and by no outside AI vendor.

What we commit to

Four commitments your security team can check.

Patient text stays with us

VLAP reads patient text on Vasl infrastructure. No patient text goes to an outside AI company or a third-party model.

HIPAA compliant

Built for protected health information from the start. We sign a business associate agreement before any patient data is shared.

Outbound texts carry no health information

Bridge and Keep check-ins say nothing about a patient’s health. Only the patient’s reply carries it.

The clinician decides

Non-device clinical decision support under the 21st Century Cures Act, Section 3060. VLAP shows its evidence and never acts on its own.
