How a pilot works

Start with one workflow. Measure it on your own patients.

A pilot runs Bridge, Keep or VLAP with your team, on your patients’ own text, against the tool you use today.

Four steps

From first call to a result you can read.

01 · Choose

Pick the workflow

Bridge from your emergency department, Keep for your members, or VLAP on text you already hold.

02 · Review

Walk the data path

Your security team reviews it with us, and we sign a business associate agreement before any patient data is shared.

03 · Run

Run it with your team

We staff Bridge with your team. Flags go to the clinicians and navigators you already have.

04 · Compare

Measure it

VLAP runs beside the tool you use today, on your own held-out text. We run the comparison with you.

What a pilot measures

Each pilot is scored on something you already track.

Vasl Bridge

The seven-day follow-up rate

The measure every Medicaid plan is scored on. Bridge records the first visit after discharge.

Vasl Keep

Who answered, and what they need

Every reply sorted, the members who need a person first, and plan-level totals.

VLAP

VLAP against your current tool

On your own held-out text, against the word list or keyword rule it would replace.
