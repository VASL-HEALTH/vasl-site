## One thing to know before reusing these

The Unsplash License covers commercial use and needs no permission, but it
grants no model release. Both photographs show identifiable people, and on
`/bridge` the photograph sits beside the line about Medicaid youth and mental
health emergency visits. Nothing on the page names the person or states that
she is a patient, and the alt text describes only what the picture shows. If
either page later adds a caption that reads as a case description, or either
photograph moves next to a claim about an individual, get a release first.
